muzikFab

muzikFab is a private, single-user desktop tool that its owner uses to manage their own YouTube music channels (for example Golden Hour Jukebox).

With the owner's permission it can:

  • upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel (always as private first),
  • set video thumbnails, titles, descriptions and playlists on that channel,
  • read the channel's own YouTube Analytics reports (views, watch time, traffic sources) to plan new videos.

muzikFab is not a public service. It is not offered to other users, it does not have user accounts, and it does not access any channel other than the one the owner explicitly authorizes.
