Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 29, 2026

muzikFab ("the app") is a private desktop tool used by a single person (the owner) to manage the owner's own YouTube channels. This policy explains what data the app accesses through Google APIs and how it is handled.

Data the app accesses

Only after the owner signs in with Google and grants permission, the app uses the following Google API scopes:

  • youtube.upload — to upload the owner's own videos to the owner's own channel;
  • youtube.readonly and youtube.force-ssl — to read and update the owner's own videos, thumbnails, playlists and comments;
  • yt-analytics.readonly — to read the owner's own channel analytics (views, watch time, traffic sources, countries, devices).

How the data is used

The data is used only to perform the actions the owner requests (uploading and managing their own videos) and to produce private analytics reports for the owner. The app does not use Google user data for advertising, does not sell it, and does not transfer it to third parties. Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Storage

OAuth tokens and generated reports are stored only on the owner's own computer. The app has no server and no database; no data is uploaded anywhere other than the owner's own YouTube channel.

Sharing

No Google user data is shared with anyone.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token files on the owner's computer also removes the app's access.
